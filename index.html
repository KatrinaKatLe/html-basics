Fairy Tail's Dragon Slayers

How Dragon Slayers came to be

Seven Dragon Slayers all from different guilds

Dragon Slayers are mages trained to use ancient magic that mimics the power of dragons. Their abilities come from being taught by dragons, implanted lacrima, or a combination of both.
